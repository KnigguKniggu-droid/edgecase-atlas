# One-minute competition video storyboard

All metrics remain `TBD` until measured after launch. Replace placeholders only with values traceable
to a test, artifact, analytics aggregate, or written pilot note.

## 0 to 8 seconds

Show a plausible driving-agent answer beside a red signal. State: "AI driving agents can sound
reasonable while violating an editable safety assumption."

## 8 to 20 seconds

Show the no-key faulty fixture and the five-property starter pack. State that properties are
operational assumptions, not universal laws or certification rules.

## 20 to 35 seconds

Start a deterministic run. Show controlled source and follow-up scenarios, the fixed seed, progress,
and charged-call count. Do not show keys, terminal paths, account identity, or private metadata.

## 35 to 50 seconds

Show a larger failing pair reduced to a `1-minimal under the declared reducer set` contrast. Display
the single retained field change, both normalized outputs, 4 of 5 engineering reproduction, and the
held-out research-confirmation boundary.

## 50 to 60 seconds

Show the canonical replay command, offline HTML export, public no-key link, and measured placeholders:
`TBD completed runs`, `TBD distinct users`, `TBD independent CLI runs`, and `TBD clarity-positive
responses`. State the implemented contribution and the next held-out research step. End on the
project name and public link.

## Recording gate

The final video must be 60 seconds or less, work without login, contain no private name, email,
affiliation, API key, local path, browser account, notification, or copied private scenario, and use
only measured metrics.
