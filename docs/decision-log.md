# Decision log

## 2026-08-11: Alpha scope

The competition alpha is limited to structured-text scenarios, five editable properties, deterministic generation, repeated evaluation, minimization, three adapters, a CLI, a Streamlit application, static reports, and a public synthetic benchmark.

CARLA, photorealistic generation, production vehicle interfaces, billing, team accounts, proprietary datasets, hosted arbitrary endpoints, and user file execution are excluded.

## 2026-08-11: Market boundary

The first user is a university autonomy lab, student robotics team, open-source driving-agent developer, or AI safety researcher. OEM validation teams are outside the initial market.

The CLI remains open source. A paid hosted plan will not be built before three recurring pilots exist.

## 2026-08-11: Research claim

The defensible contribution is the integrated method of typed counterfactual relations, constraint preservation, decision-boundary coverage, stochastic reproduction, and hierarchical reduction for black-box driving reasoning agents.

The alpha reports a 1-minimal reproducing contrast under its declared reducer set. It does not claim a uniquely causal explanation, global minimum, formal proof, or real-world safety evidence.

The project will not claim to be the first scenario generator, fuzzer, counterfactual driving benchmark, or delta-debugging system.

## 2026-08-11: Safety and privacy

The tool is for simulated research and debugging. Its properties are editable operational assumptions, not universal law, certification criteria, or proof of real-world safety.

Public artifacts use a project pseudonym and anonymous noreply identity. Private autonomy research remains unchanged and outside the repository.

## 2026-08-11: Deployment gate

Local implementation and verification may proceed immediately. Public push and Streamlit deployment require a verified anonymous GitHub account or organization. Competition submission requires final user approval. Payment and paid-program enrollment are outside authorization.

## 2026-08-16: Release verification

The local 0.1.0 release candidate uses one Python 3.12 verifier for privacy scanning, linting, type checking, tests, bounded no-key smoke checks, deterministic checksums, and package construction. CI runs the same verifier.

Generated evidence remains ignored unless it is deliberately curated and passes the identity scanner. Local verification never authorizes a public push, deployment, outreach, competition submission, or payment.
