# One-minute competition video storyboard

All metrics remain unresolved until measured after launch. Use only values traceable to a test,
analytics aggregate, artifact, or consented product-feedback record.

## 0 to 8 seconds

Show a plausible driving-agent answer beside a red signal. Narration: "AI driving agents can sound
reasonable while violating an editable safety assumption."

## 8 to 20 seconds

Show the no-key faulty fixture and five-property starter pack. Narration: "EdgeCase Atlas tests five
operational assumptions with a built-in faulty agent, without an API key. These are debugging
properties, not certification rules."

## 20 to 35 seconds

Start a deterministic run. Show controlled scenarios, fixed seed, progress, and call count.
Narration: "It generates valid paired counterfactuals, repeats stochastic checks, and records every
target call under a fixed budget."

## 35 to 50 seconds

Show a large pair reduced to a `1-minimal under the declared reducer set` contrast. Display the one
retained field change, normalized outputs, 4-of-5 engineering reproduction, and held-out research
boundary. Narration: "Atlas reduces the failure to one causal change, then accepts an engineering
certificate only when it reproduces at least four times in five runs."

## 50 to 60 seconds

Show the replay command, offline report, public link, and only evidence-backed metrics. Narration
template: "Each certificate has a replay command and offline report. [RUNS] runs from [USERS] users
now guide the next matched-method and simulator study."

## Recording gate

The final video must be at most 60.0 seconds, work without login, and contain no private identity,
email, affiliation, API key, local path, account metadata, private scenario, or unsupported metric.
